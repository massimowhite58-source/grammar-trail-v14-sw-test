# Dashboard, Knowledge Map & Progress

## Dashboard
Welcome Header; Learning Overview; central MAMI Recommendation; Skills (Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading, Writing); Learning Activity; Knowledge Map Preview; Recent Activity.
Support New User, Active User, Returning After Break and No Data states. Use only real engine data; figures in conceptual examples are not product metrics.

## Knowledge Map
Interactive concept/topic map with nodes, dependencies, statuses, topic details and navigation to relevant material.
Relationship types: Prerequisite, Related, Part of, Reinforces.
Statuses: Not Started, Learning, Developing, Mastered, Needs Review.
Topic detail: Topic, Mastery, History, Related Topics, Difficulties, Recommendation. Recommendation explanations require real engine signals.

## Progress
Learning Overview, Skills, Learning Activity, Mastery Trend. Activity and Mastery Trend are separate charts. Periods: 7 days, 30 days, All time.

## Mastery rules — not approved
Mastery Score formula, status thresholds and Needs Review algorithm are not defined by this design spec. They belong to the learning engine. Never infer mastery from one percentage of correct answers.
