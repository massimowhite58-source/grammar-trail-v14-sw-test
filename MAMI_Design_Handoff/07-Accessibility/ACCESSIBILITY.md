# Accessibility Requirements

- Full keyboard navigation and visible, logically ordered focus states.
- Semantic labels, roles and states for screen readers.
- Never rely on color alone for status or errors.
- Verify text, icon, border and focus contrast in Dark and Light.
- Comfortable mobile touch targets and forms.
- Support prefers-reduced-motion; essential information must not depend on animation.
- Zoom to 200%, long labels and localization must not break layout.
- Validation feedback must be announced and associated with relevant fields.
