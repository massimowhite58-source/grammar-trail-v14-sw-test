# Brand Identity — approved direction

## Brand
- **MAMI — Massimo Mega Intelligence**.
- Technology / IT; adaptive language-learning platform.
- Modern, bold, technological, innovative, premium and confident.

## Logo
- Approved mark: flowing gold ribbon forming an M, with the MAMI wordmark.
- Do not redesign or alter the approved logo's proportions, geometry, composition or character.
- Rejected concept: Adaptive M with blue/cyan/gold form — do not use.
- Refined ribbon geometry v0.2 was approved on 19 November 2026 for Animated Entry. This reconstructed SVG symbol does not replace the original full brand lockup from the Brand Kit where the complete logo is required.

## App icon and palette
- Primary icon: gold symbol on dark navy.
- Alternative: dark symbol on gold.
- Primary colors: Navy and Gold; exact starting tokens are in Foundation CSS.
- Dark is primary; Light is alternative.

## Usage
- Use approved vector sources.
- Do not add slogans or advertising phrases.
- Do not use superseded v0.1 reconstruction or preliminary concepts.
- For a complete production lockup in other formats, use original artwork from the Brand Kit or obtain vector artwork from the brand owner; do not upscale a small raster and claim it is vector source.
